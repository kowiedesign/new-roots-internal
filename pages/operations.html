Operational Transition

What excites us

The owner staying on changes the central question from "can the business survive him leaving" to "can we support him and the staff well enough that operations stay stable and improve" — an easier starting point than a typical acquisition.

What could go wrong

Key-person risk is reduced, not eliminated — it shifts to his bandwidth, health, and whether an employee role actually suits him long-term, even if it solves his visa problem on paper.

What We Still Need to Learn

  • Staff roles, visa categories, and retention
  • Lease terms — currently almost entirely unknown (landlord, length, assignability)
  • Licenses and their transferability
  • Supplier relationships
  • Whether the restaurant's reputation is tied to the owner personally or to something more transferable

Mistakes to Guard Against

  • Trusting seller-provided numbers without independent verification
  • Underestimating post-acquisition working capital needs
  • Assuming goodwill transfers automatically with ownership
  • Letting "the owner needs a visa solution" quietly stand in for "the business is healthy" — different facts, easy to conflate given this deal's emotional pull
